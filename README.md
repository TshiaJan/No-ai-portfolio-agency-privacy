# Privacy Policy — No AI Portfolio

**Effective date:** September 30, 2026
**Developer:** Tshiamo Jantjie — janaire.dev@gmail.com

No AI Portfolio ("the app") is an offline stock and holdings tracker built on a simple principle: we do not collect, transmit, or sell your data, because the app is not technically able to.

## 1. Data we collect
**None.** The app requests no permissions and contains no network code. It cannot connect to the internet, cannot send data anywhere, and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

## 2. Data stored on your device
The app stores the holdings you enter — ticker symbol, number of shares, price paid, purchase date, and current price — in a local database (`noaiportfolio.db`) and preferences (`noai_prefs`) on your device only. This data:

- never leaves your device;
- is not backed up by us (device backups, if you enable them, are handled by your device's OS settings);
- can be deleted at any time by deleting a holding in the app, or uninstalled (which removes the local database).

## 3. No AI, no profiling, no tracking
The app contains no artificial intelligence, no health scores, no predictions, no profiling, and no third-party tracking. Gain/loss figures are simple arithmetic on the numbers you enter.

## 4. Purchases
The app offers a one-time, non-subscription in-app purchase ($3.99) that unlocks unlimited holdings. The purchase is processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Play's own policies apply to the transaction. Your unlock status is stored locally on your device.

## 5. Children's privacy
The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13, and is rated for general audiences.

## 6. Permissions
The app declares **zero permissions** in its manifest. It has no access to the internet, storage, camera, microphone, or location.

## 7. Changes
If a future version ever changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the change will be disclosed here before release.
